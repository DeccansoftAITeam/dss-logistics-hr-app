# Severance and Separation Guidelines (Version 2.0)

> **CONFIDENTIAL & RESTRICTED**: This document contains sensitive compensation formulas and organizational restructuring procedures reserved exclusively for HR Business Partners and HR Leadership.

## 1. Scope and Objective
This document outlines standard separation benefits, severance calculation frameworks, and release protocols applied during organizational restructuring, redundancy programs, or mutually agreed separations at DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd.

## 2. Standard Severance Calculation Formula
For involuntary separations resulting from role redundancy or organizational restructuring (excluding terminations for cause):
1. **Severance Pay**: Calculated at the rate of one (1.0) month of basic monthly salary for each completed continuous year of service at DSS Logistics.
2. **Severance Cap**: The total severance payout is capped at a maximum of 12 months of basic salary.
3. **Gratuity & Statutory Settlements**: Statutory gratuity and earned leave encashment (up to maximum carry-forward limits) are disbursed separately in compliance with regional labor laws.
4. **Healthcare Continuation**: Medical insurance coverage is continued for 60 calendar days post the official separation date.

## 3. Mandatory Escalation for Personal Severance Inquiries
Severance packages and mutual separation agreements involve complex legal covenants, non-disclosure obligations, and individual tax implications.

Under no circumstances should automated assistants, bots, or individual managers advise an employee whether to accept, reject, or negotiate a severance offer. Any employee inquiry regarding acceptance of a severance package, mutual separation terms, or legal release terms must be escalated immediately to HR Operations and the Employee Relations separation committee.
