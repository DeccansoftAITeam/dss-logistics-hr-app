# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose and Scope
DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd is committed to maintaining the highest ethical standards across all our global and regional operations. This Code of Conduct applies to all full-time, part-time, and contract personnel worldwide.

## 2. Professional Ethics & Integrity
Every employee represents DSS Logistics in their interactions with clients, vendors, logistics partners, and fellow colleagues. Honest communication, transparency, and respect are mandatory at all times. Fraud, misrepresentation, or falsification of operational logs will result in immediate investigation.

## 3. Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid any personal interest, relationship, or investment that could conflict with their duties at DSS Logistics. Engaging in secondary employment or consulting with competitors or freight suppliers without written approval from HR Operations is strictly prohibited.

## 4. Equal Opportunity and Workplace Safety
DSS Logistics enforces equal opportunity employment regardless of race, gender, religion, caste, sexual orientation, disability, or age. Workplace harassment, discrimination, or physical intimidation will not be tolerated.

## 5. Reporting Violations & Whistleblower Protection
Employees who observe ethical or safety violations should report them to HR Operations or use the anonymous Ethics Hotline (ethics@dsslogistics.example). DSS Logistics maintains a strict non-retaliation policy for all good-faith disclosures.
