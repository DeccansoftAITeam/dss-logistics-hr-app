# IT Acceptable Use Policy (Version 5.2)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy defines the acceptable standards for utilizing computer systems, communication networks, mobile devices, and digital assets owned or managed by DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd. Compliance is mandatory for all personnel.

## 2. Authentication, Passwords, and Multi-Factor Authentication
1. Passwords must be at least 14 characters in length, containing at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special symbol.
2. Passwords must be updated every 90 days. Reusing the previous 5 passwords is prohibited.
3. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via company-approved authenticator apps or hardware tokens is mandatory for accessing any DSS corporate system or cloud environment.

## 3. Network and VPN Usage
When connecting to DSS corporate systems from external locations (home networks, client sites, hotels, airports), employees must always connect through the corporate Virtual Private Network (VPN) using full-tunnel encryption. Direct access without VPN to internal databases, freight management consoles, or ERP tools is blocked.

## 4. Software Installation and Generative AI Tools
1. Employees may not install third-party software, browser plugins, or unapproved applications on DSS laptops without explicit written approval and deployment via the IT Service Portal.
2. Employees are strictly prohibited from uploading confidential corporate data, customer shipment logs, personally identifiable information (PII), or proprietary software code into public, unauthorized generative AI chatbots or public LLM services. Only internal, corporate-sanctioned AI tools (such as DSS Ask Policy) may be utilized for company business.

## 5. Device Security and Encryption
All DSS-issued workstations and mobile devices are configured with full-disk encryption (BitLocker/FileVault). Employees must never disable antivirus agents, enterprise monitoring tools, or firewall configurations. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security (it-security@dsslogistics.example) within two hours.
