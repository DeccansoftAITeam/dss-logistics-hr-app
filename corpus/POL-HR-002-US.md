# Leave Policy — US Addendum (Version 1.1)

## 1. Statutory Context & Scope
This regional addendum supplements the Corporate Leave Policy (POL-HR-002 v3.0) for all full-time and part-time personnel employed by DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd within the United States (including the Chicago Logistics Center and regional distribution offices).

## 2. Paid Sick Leave in the US
Full-time US employees are provided with 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.0 day per full calendar month worked. Paid sick leave may be utilized for personal medical appointments, preventative healthcare, illness, or caring for an immediate family member. Medical certification is required if an employee is absent for more than three consecutive working days.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) and Vacation Accrual
In lieu of standard privilege leave, US employees accrue 15 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per calendar year. Up to a maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year, expiring on March 31st of that following year if not taken.

## 4. US Public and Federal Holidays
US employees receive 10 paid federal holidays annually as scheduled in the DSS Logistics Holiday Calendar (CAL-2026). In addition, full-time US staff receive 2 floating holidays per calendar year.
