# Travel & Expense Policy (Version 3.0)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the principles and reimbursement limits governing official business travel, lodging, and daily meal allowances for employees of DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd across all operating geographies.

## 2. Travel Authorization and Bookings
All official travel must be pre-approved by the department head via the corporate travel portal at least 7 days prior to departure. Flights, rail bookings, and authorized hotel accommodations must be booked through DSS Logistics' designated travel management desk.

## 3. Domestic Travel and Daily Allowances
Employees traveling domestically on corporate business are eligible for a per diem allowance covering daily meals and local incidental transport. 

### 3.1 Metro and Tier-1 City Caps
For domestic business travel within India:
- Tier-1 Metros (New Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad): The combined daily per-diem allowance cap is ₹6,000 per day.
- Mumbai Special Rate: Due to standard national pricing caps, the corporate daily per-diem cap for lodging and meals in Mumbai is strictly fixed at ₹5,000 per day.
- Tier-2 and Tier-3 Cities: The daily allowance cap is ₹3,500 per day.

## 4. International Travel
Employees traveling internationally receive lodging booked directly by DSS Global Operations and an international daily meal stipend of $75 USD per day.

## 5. Expense Submission and Audit
All expense reports must be submitted through the corporate expense management tool within 15 working days following the conclusion of the trip. Original receipts, boarding passes, and GST invoices are required for all non-per-diem expenditures.
