# DSS Logistics Official Holiday Calendar 2026

## 1. Overview
This calendar lists the official statutory and festive holidays observed by DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd for the calendar year 2026. Holiday observance is determined by the employee's designated primary operating region.

---

## 2. India Operations Holiday Schedule (10 Days)
The following 10 public holidays are observed by all employees based in India (Hyderabad, Bangalore, Mumbai, and regional freight facilities):

1. **Republic Day**: Monday, January 26, 2026 (National Mandatory)
2. **Holi**: Wednesday, March 25, 2026
3. **Eid-ul-Fitr**: Friday, March 20, 2026
4. **May Day (Labor Day)**: Friday, May 1, 2026
5. **Independence Day**: Saturday, August 15, 2026 (National Mandatory)
6. **Vinayaka Chaturthi**: Monday, September 14, 2026
7. **Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti**: Friday, October 2, 2026 (National Mandatory)
8. **Dussehra (Vijayadashami)**: Tuesday, October 20, 2026
9. **Diwali (Deepavali)**: Sunday, November 8, 2026
10. **Christmas Day**: Friday, December 25, 2026
