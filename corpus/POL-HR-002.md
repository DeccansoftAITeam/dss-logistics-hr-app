# Corporate Leave Policy (Version 3.0)

## 1. Overview
This policy establishes the standard leave guidelines and annual leave entitlements for all full-time employees of DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd. Regional addenda outline local statutory supplements.

## 2. Annual Leave and Privilege Leave
Employees accrue 20 days of paid annual privilege leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at the rate of 1.66 days per completed month of active service. Leaves must be requested at least two weeks in advance via the HR self-service portal.

## 3. Sick and Medical Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of paid sick leave per calendar year to recover from illness or medical incapacitation. A certified medical practitioner certificate is required for continuous absences exceeding three consecutive working days. Unused sick leave cannot be encashed at separation.

## 4. Carry-Forward and Accumulation
Annual leave balances may be accumulated subject to organizational operational limits. An employee may carry forward a maximum of 10 unused privilege leave days into the subsequent calendar year, and any excess unused days beyond this 10-day cap expire automatically on December 31st without monetary compensation.

## 5. Parental and Compassionate Leave
Maternity leave covers 26 weeks of fully paid leave for eligible mothers. Paternity leave provides 2 weeks of paid leave within six months of child arrival. Bereavement leave provides up to 5 consecutive paid days for immediate family members.
