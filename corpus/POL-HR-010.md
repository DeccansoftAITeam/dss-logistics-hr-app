# Internal Disciplinary Procedure & Guidelines (Version 3.1)

> **CONFIDENTIAL & RESTRICTED**: This document is strictly intended for HR Managers and designated Employee Relations Officers. Distribution or disclosure to non-HR personnel is prohibited.

## 1. Purpose and Philosophy
This framework provides HR Managers with procedural guidance for managing employee misconduct, performance negligence, and ethical violations in a fair, standardized, and legally defensible manner across DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd.

## 2. Progressive Disciplinary Steps
Disciplinary actions follow a progressive ladder unless gross misconduct warrants immediate escalated action:

### 2.1 Step 1: Informal Counseling
Informal discussion between the manager and employee to highlight performance or minor conduct discrepancies. Documented in the manager’s internal 1-on-1 notes; not logged on the central HR personnel record.

### 2.2 Step 2: First Written Reprimand
Issued when informal counseling fails to correct conduct, or for intermediate policy violations (such as unauthorized absenteeism exceeding 3 days). Remains active in the employee’s personnel file for 6 months.

### 2.3 Step 3: Final Written Warning & Inquiry Hearing
Issued for repeated infractions or serious misconduct (unauthorized system access, verbal aggression). The employee is placed on a 30-day monitoring period with suspension of promotion and discretionary bonus eligibility.

### 2.4 Step 4: Suspension Pending Investigation
For allegations involving fraud, financial misappropriation, or harassment, the employee is suspended on paid administrative leave pending an investigation by the Internal Inquiry Committee.

### 2.5 Step 5: Separation / Termination
Termination of employment approved by the Head of HR Operations and General Legal Counsel following conclusive investigative findings.

## 3. Mandatory Documentation Standards
HR Managers must record all statements, evidentiary logs, and witness notes in the secure HRIS Employee Relations vault. Incomplete or verbal-only disciplinary actions will not be recognized in statutory labor forums.
