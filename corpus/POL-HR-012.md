# Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Handbook (Version 1.3)

## 1. Purpose of Performance Improvement Plans
At DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd, a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is designed as a constructive tool to assist employees who are not meeting established performance benchmarks. The objective is to provide structured coaching, clear milestones, and measurable targets to help the employee return to successful standing.

## 2. Initiation Criteria and Timelines
A formal PIP is initiated only after documented informal coaching has failed to yield sufficient progress over a 30-day period. 

Key parameters of the program:
1. **Standard Duration**: A standard PIP runs for a fixed term of 60 calendar days. In exceptional operational environments, a 30-day expedited PIP may be approved by the Department Head.
2. **Bi-Weekly Reviews**: The employee and direct manager must conduct documented formal review sessions every 14 days to track deliverables against predefined SMART milestones.
3. **HR BP Participation**: The designated HR Business Partner attends the initial onboarding meeting, the 30-day midpoint review, and the final 60-day closure review.

## 3. Measurable Milestones & Objectives
Every PIP document must specify:
- Clear performance deficits with reference to job description SLAs (e.g. freight dispatch turnaround times, customer invoice dispute resolution rates).
- Specific, measurable outcomes required for successful completion.
- Required training resources, mentor assignments, or tool access provided by DSS Logistics.

## 4. Outcomes and Conclusion
At the end of the 60-day period:
- **Successful Completion**: If all milestones are met, the employee is returned to good standing. The PIP record is archived.
- **Extension**: If substantial progress is demonstrated with minor pending deliverables, a one-time 30-day extension may be granted.
- **Unsuccessful Completion**: If the employee fails to meet the agreed milestones, employment will be subject to progressive disciplinary review, including possible reassignment or termination of employment.
