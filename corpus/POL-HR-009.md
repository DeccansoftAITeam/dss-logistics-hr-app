# Anti-Harassment and Non-Discrimination Policy (Version 2.0)

## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy
DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and dignified work environment free from sexual harassment, discrimination, verbal abuse, cyber-bullying, and intimidation. This policy protects all employees, contractors, interns, and visitors across all workplaces, corporate gatherings, and digital communication channels.

## 2. Prohibited Behaviors
Harassment includes, but is not limited to:
- Unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, or inappropriate physical touching.
- Derogatory, offensive, or disparaging remarks regarding gender, race, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
- Displaying offensive imagery, suggestive posters, or circulating inappropriate jokes via email, messaging platforms, or social media.
- Creating a hostile work environment through micro-aggressions, isolation, or bullying.

## 3. Mandatory Escalation and Formal Grievance Mechanism
Personal harassment incidents, safety concerns, or retaliation allegations cannot and must not be resolved via self-service automated tools or generic policy lookup bots. 

Any employee experiencing or witnessing harassment must immediately lodge a confidential report directly with:
1. Internal Complaints Committee (POSH Committee) for India-based employees: posh-committee@dsslogistics.example.
2. Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) Compliance Officer for US and global personnel: eeo-officer@dsslogistics.example.
3. HR Operations Escalation Desk: hr-escalations@dsslogistics.example.

All formal complaints are investigated within 72 hours under strict confidentiality. DSS Logistics guarantees complete non-retaliation protection for any employee who raises a concern in good faith.
