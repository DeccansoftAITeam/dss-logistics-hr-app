# Corporate Leave Policy (Version 2.0 - Superseded)

## 1. Overview
[SUPERSEDED ARCHIVE] Historical policy document superseded on 2026-04-01 by POL-HR-002 v3.0.

## 2. Annual Leave and Privilege Leave
Employees accrue 18 days of paid annual privilege leave per calendar year.

## 3. Sick and Medical Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A doctor's certificate is required for absences exceeding four days.

## 4. Carry-Forward Limits
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused privilege leave days into the next calendar year. Excess leave lapses on December 31st.
