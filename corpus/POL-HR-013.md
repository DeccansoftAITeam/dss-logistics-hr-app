# Employee Benefits FAQ (Version 2.4)

## 1. Group Medical & Health Insurance
**Q: What is the medical coverage provided by DSS Logistics?**  
A: DSS Logistics provides comprehensive Group Medical Cover (GMC) of ₹5,00,000 per annum for India-based employees (family floater covering employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children). For US-based personnel, the company sponsors 85% of premium costs for comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance via the designated group health plan ($10,000 annual family stop-loss limit).

## 2. Wellness and Fitness Allowance
**Q: Does DSS Logistics offer a fitness or gym reimbursement?**  
A: Yes. All full-time employees are eligible for a monthly wellness and fitness stipend of ₹1,000 per month (or $25 USD for US-based staff). This covers gym memberships, yoga classes, or sports club subscriptions. Claims are submitted quarterly through the employee perks portal.

## 3. Annual Health Checkups
**Q: Are preventative executive health checkups covered?**  
A: All permanent employees over the age of 30 are entitled to one complimentary annual health screening package at our network diagnostic partner centers.

## 4. Dependent Care & Parental Benefits
**Q: What dependent support is available for working parents?**  
A: DSS Logistics provides tie-ups with daycare centers near major distribution offices and a monthly childcare subsidy of ₹3,000 per child (up to age 6) for on-site logistics operators.
