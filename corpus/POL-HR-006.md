# Remote Work & Equipment Policy (Version 2.1)

## 1. Objective and Applicability
DSS Logistics Pvt Ltd supports flexible working arrangements that balance operational demands with employee well-being. This policy applies to all global personnel seeking remote or hybrid work accommodations.

## 2. Manager Discretion and Team Coverage
Working remotely is a privilege, not an automatic statutory entitlement. Approval for any remote work arrangement rests with the employee's direct Reporting Manager and Department Head. Approval is contingent on maintaining operational SLAs, warehouse coverage, freight dispatch coordination, and client support responsiveness. Managers retain the discretion to revoke remote work authorization with two weeks' notice if performance benchmarks or team attendance requirements are unmet.

## 3. Role Eligibility and Probation Criteria
To be considered for hybrid or full remote work:
1. The employee's designated job code must be formally classified as "Remote Eligible" within the Human Resources Information System (HRIS). Frontline warehouse operations, freight yard dispatchers, and physical security roles are categorized as "On-Site Mandatory" and are strictly ineligible.
2. The employee must have successfully completed their mandatory 90-day probationary period.
3. The employee must have received a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher in their most recent quarterly evaluation.

Because eligibility depends upon both role classification in Section 3 and managerial operational clearance in Section 2, employees should consult their manager and check their HRIS role status before submitting an application.

## 4. Work Equipment and Infrastructure Allowance

### 4.1 Company-Provided Hardware
DSS Logistics IT provides every full-time employee with a standard encrypted laptop, charger, and hardware security token.

### 4.2 One-Time Remote Setup Allowance
Full-time employees approved for hybrid or remote arrangements are eligible for a one-time remote work equipment allowance of ₹25,000 (or $300 USD for US-based staff). This allowance may be utilized for purchasing ergonomic desk chairs, external monitors, keyboards, mice, and desk accessories.

### 4.3 Claim Submission and Reimbursement Window
Equipment reimbursement claims must be submitted through the internal expense portal within 30 days of the purchase date, accompanied by itemized tax invoices. Non-tax receipts or claims submitted after 30 days will be rejected.

### 4.4 Monthly Internet & Connectivity Reimbursement
Eligible remote and hybrid employees may claim a monthly internet reimbursement allowance of up to ₹1,500 per month (or $30 USD for US staff) to support broadband high-speed connectivity. Monthly broadband bills must be submitted through the payroll portal by the 25th of each active billing cycle.
